## Gate tools

The python-uv profile runs [unclebob/crapper](https://github.com/unclebob/crapper), [unclebob/dryer](https://github.com/unclebob/dryer) and [unclebob/mutator](https://github.com/unclebob/mutator), all 0BSD. They are cloned at pinned commits on first use, not vendored.
